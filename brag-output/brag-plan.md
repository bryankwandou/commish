# /brag plan: Commish

**What it is.** A Solana program and app that locks an affiliate commission in USDC the moment a sale is recorded and pays it once the refund window closes.
**For whom.** Brands that pay creators per sale, and the creators waiting to be paid.
**What sets it apart.** The money is reserved on-chain, where the brand can't spend it, and anyone can trigger the payout. A creator who can't wait sells the pending commission for cash.
**Most impressive claim.** The whole protocol is one 9,296-byte program with 26 tests that attack it.
**Visual hook.** The site's own headline, "Commissions that pay themselves", set big, then the mark's arc sweeping from amber (held) to green (paid).
**Real UI shown.** The landing page's commission card (record the sale, let the window close) and the early-payout calculator, driven live in the running app.
**Tone.** Polished: serious, restrained, long holds, soft transitions that dip through the background.
**Share caption.** One line: affiliate commissions held in USDC on Solana until the refund window closes, then paid by anyone.
