## Data that cuts against Commish

- **S7.** At least eight earlier Solana affiliate or referral projects entered Colosseum hackathons; none placed. Three (SuperLink, Sendit, Redio) promised instant payouts, which ignores returns (S2): a brand cannot pay a commission on an order that may be refunded. Commish holds the commission through the refund window instead, so it matches how brands already pay.
- **S1.** The biggest affiliate program already pays by direct deposit on a fixed schedule. The pain is the wait and the lack of a guarantee, not whether the money arrives.
- **S3** is a forecast from a marketing research firm, read through a vendor's blog. Treat it as an order of magnitude.
- **S8** covers high-risk verticals only, and is read through a third-party article.
- Commish trusts the brand's attestor to report real sales. It cannot prove an off-chain order happened.
